About IGNITE ALGERIA

IGNITE ALGERIA is a premier energy-sector competition organized by Scientific Corner Ouargla - CFC Club at Kasdi Merbah University.

This three-day event gathers students, engineers, and professionals to tackle challenges in the oil and gas industry.

Participants engage in competitions, workshops, and networking sessions to present innovative solutions to industry experts.
